# Where are there opportunities for directs?\n\n## Short answer\n\n**Not as a broad network push for customer speed.** Same SoCal constrained origin × dest, directs are slower on O2D actual in every major region over May–Aug. Opportunities are **narrow, conditional, and mostly operational/network** — not a general O2D win.\n\n## What the data already ruled out\n\n| Claim | Result |\n|-------|--------|\n| Directs beat local on O2D actual (same OD) | **No** — all major dests slower (+0.2d to +1.1d) |\n| Directs beat own BAU local in high-vol week | **No** — +0.8d slower vol-weighted |\n| Directs free hub capacity (higher in-hub IFR) | **Not proven** — hub corr −0.69 |\n| Directs disproportionately cause cushions | **Weak** — only ~7% of cushioned vol; IFR vs del gap is real though |\n\n## Where narrow opportunities still exist\n\n### 1) High-vol / constrained stress weeks — same-week mitigation (not BAU-beating)\n\nIn Memorial Day, directs were **~0.2d faster than same-week local** overall. That is relief vs a stressed local path, not faster than normal BAU. Use as a **surge playbook**, not everyday policy.\n\n| Dest | Direct vol | Local vol | Direct O2D | Local O2D | Δ |\n|------|------------|-----------|------------|-----------|---|\n| Southeast | 15,662 | 748 | 7.92 | 10.06 | -2.14 |\n| Northeast | 10,501 | 914 | 8.32 | 9.87 | -1.55 |\n| South Central | 11,689 | 876 | 5.60 | 7.11 | -1.51 |\n| Midwest | 20,806 | 2,610 | 7.92 | 8.57 | -0.65 |\n| Mountain | 13,195 | 9,983 | 4.82 | 4.01 | +0.81 |\n| West Coast | 21,252 | 36,097 | 4.76 | 3.50 | +1.26 |\n\n### 2) Specific week × dest pockets (direct ≥0.15d faster)\n\n**8** dest×week cells clear the bar (material vol).\n\n| Dest | Week | Direct vol | Local vol | Δ O2D | Direct IFR | Local IFR |\n|------|------|------------|-----------|-------|------------|-----------|\n| Midwest | 2026-05-31 | 15,105 | 2,056 | -1.44 | 88% | 80% |\n| Southeast | 2026-06-07 | 9,734 | 1,879 | -1.39 | 93% | 77% |\n| Northeast | 2026-06-07 | 6,479 | 1,690 | -1.00 | 93% | 77% |\n| Midwest | 2026-06-07 | 12,132 | 3,710 | -0.88 | 92% | 82% |\n| South Central | 2026-06-07 | 7,402 | 1,738 | -0.78 | 94% | 82% |\n| Midwest | 2026-05-24 | 20,806 | 2,610 | -0.65 | 83% | 80% |\n| Northeast | 2026-05-17 | 8,228 | 1,244 | -0.49 | 90% | 90% |\n| Southeast | 2026-05-17 | 11,699 | 1,327 | -0.46 | 91% | 90% |\n\n### 3) STO × dest niches\n\n**5** STO×dest pairs where directs are ≥0.15d faster (May–Aug).\n\n| Dest | STO | Direct vol | Local vol | Δ O2D | Direct IFR |\n|------|-----|------------|-----------|-------|------------|\n| Mid-Atlantic | Outdoor Furniture | 1,587 | 2,875 | -0.53 | 86% |\n| Northeast | Outdoor Furniture | 2,043 | 5,553 | -0.43 | 85% |\n| Midwest | Outdoor Furniture | 3,662 | 8,633 | -0.32 | 84% |\n| South Central | Outdoor Furniture | 1,354 | 5,755 | -0.31 | 83% |\n| Southeast | Outdoor Furniture | 2,171 | 5,620 | -0.21 | 85% |\n\n### 4) FedEx / network value (not supplier O2D)\n\nDirects still cut post-induction miles (~80–100 vs ~1,300–2,000). If FedEx values **constrained SoCal hub relief / linehaul**, that can be an opportunity — but it should be sold as a **network KPI**, not “faster to customer.” Pair with induction SLA so IFR does not crater into cushions.\n\n### 5) Clean-up opportunities (anti-directs)\n\nGhost warehouses and misshipping muddy “direct” volume and hurt reliability. Cleaning those improves any future directs program more than pushing more volume.\n\n## Recommended posture\n\n| Do | Don’t |\n|----|-------|\n| Pilot 1–2 **surge** lanes in high-vol weeks with O2D success bar vs same-week local | Broad supplier push on miles/`direct_gain` |\n| Pre-agree IFR dip tolerance + det delivery floor | Promise customers/suppliers faster O2D from directs |\n| Chase STO×dest niches that already win in data | Assume capacity IFR lift without measuring in-hub IFR |\n| Fix ghost/misshipping | Count jumbo/ghost as “directs success” |\n\n## Files\n\n- `directs_opportunity_by_dest.csv`\n- `directs_opportunity_winning_weeks.csv`\n- `directs_opportunity_by_sto_dest.csv`\n- `directs_opportunity_memorial_by_dest.csv`\n
