## Why this matters

As peak approaches quickly, feedback from FedEx has been that **constrained markets** have limited ability to add ad hoc trailers and guarantee on-time inductions (before 8 AM next day).

In order to have success in peak and Q3 sales, suppliers might look towards **weekend shipping**, **building directs**, and **splitting conveyable and non-conveyable items**.

Suppliers should continue to provide accurate forecasts to FedEx and leverage the weekly forecast send.

**Pro tip:** We recommend scheduling a call with your suppliers during the **Sprint Window** (August 3rd – August 14th) to align on performance and finalize these plans.
